Magenda Drive hand-off checklist

The steps only you can do for the end-of-day hand-off: Finish Day uploads the day to Google Drive and starts your Claude routine. Nothing here blocks the implementation. Every phase builds against fakes. Do these once the feature branch is complete, before its pull request merges.

Before implementation: nothing to do.

After implementation

1. Turn on Google Drive for your OAuth client

≈ 5 min
ma1-google-cloud-drive

Magenda now asks for drive.file, which reaches only the files Magenda creates. Without the API and the scope, sign-in or the upload fails.

Report: ma1 done: Drive API enabled, drive.file on the consent screen

2. Adapt your end-of-day skill and create its routine

≈ 45 min
ma2-routine-and-skill

The routine lives outside this repository. It only works with Magenda once your skill reads the payload and writes the status lines described in the contract.

Report: ma2 done: skill updated (commit <sha>), routine trig_<id> with API trigger. Report only the trigger ID, never the token.

3. Sign in again and connect the routine on the iPad

≈ 10 min
ma3-ipad-sign-in-and-routine

Agreeing to the new scope and storing the token in the Keychain can only happen on a real device.

Report: ma3 done: signed in with three scopes, routine saved

4. Take one real day from Finish Day to Closed

≈ 30 min, plus the routine's run time
ma4-real-day-end-to-end

This is the only check against the real Drive, Docs, routine and PrivacyFence. It also shows whether your handwriting reads well in the PDF.

Report: ma4 pass|fail: upload, Doc, notes, (summary,) closure; handwriting legible yes|no; notes: …

5. Finish a day offline

≈ 10 min
ma5-offline-finish

The queue has to keep the day while offline and send it once you are back.

Report: ma5 pass|fail: queued offline, sent on return

6. Check the App Store privacy answers

≈ 10 min
ma6-privacy-answers

Day content now leaves the device for your Google Drive and your Claude routine, when you ask. The privacy answers have to stay true before the next TestFlight build.

Report: ma6 done: privacy answers unchanged | updated to …
